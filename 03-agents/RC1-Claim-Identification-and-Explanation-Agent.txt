RC1 - Claim Identification & Explanation Agent
Specialized Agent. Export: RC1-Claim-Identification-and-Explanation-Agent.json
The full prompt is in the export (prompt_template), along with the output schema and
the quality criteria configured for this agent.

ROLE
Finds the regulated claims in a draft and checks every cited claim ID against the
approved records with one call to validate_approved_claim. Explains each result in
plain language.

INPUT
draft_content, market, audience (required); as_of_date, cited_claim_ids (optional).
The workflow's IN1 step pulls the cited claim IDs out of the draft.

OUTPUT
JSON: validation_status, all_valid, one result per cited claim (tool outcome, reasons,
approved wording, required disclaimer, whether the draft's wording matches, and an
explanation), plus claim statements that carry no claim ID.

TOOLS
validate_approved_claim (custom tool).

BOUNDARY
RC1 does not set the readiness state or approve content. It passes market, audience
and date to the tool exactly as given and never invents a claim ID.
